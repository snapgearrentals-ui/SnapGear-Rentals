Terms & Conditions

Please read these before booking. These requirements help keep every rental safe and secure.

01

Valid IDs

Provide 2 valid IDs and a selfie with them for verification.

02

Signed Agreement

A rental contract must be signed by the ID holder.

03

Proof of Address

Submit a billing statement matching the name on your ID.

04

Reservation Fee

₱200 reservation fee is non-refundable but deductible from the total payment.

05

Security Deposit

₱1,000 security deposit is refundable after camera return and inspection.

06

Video Verification

A short video call showing your ID is required before pick-up.

07

Facebook Verification

The renter must first be mutually connected with the owner on Facebook and add the owner for verification.
